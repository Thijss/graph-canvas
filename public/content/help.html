Visual editors

Use Visual mode to build your graph with editable rows. Each editor also has a Text mode for direct editing.

Edges

An edge connects a From node to a To node. Set its color, line style, and optional label in the row.

Boundaries and groups

Boundaries organize nodes inside a colored hull. Groups color their member nodes without adding a hull.

Nodes can belong to multiple boundaries or groups.

Canvas modes

Physics arranges connected nodes automatically. DAG lays out directed graphs in levels. Custom lets you position nodes freely.

Use the canvas toggles to show or hide directions, boundaries, and groups.

Dark mode

Click the logo in the top-left corner to switch between light and dark mode. Your choice is saved for your next visit.
